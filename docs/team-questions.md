Post this in the announcements channel of the "SkinVaults 2.0" category. The feedback channel next to it has the buttons
that open a private ticket, so the message points people there.

---

## SkinVaults 2.0: we are rebuilding, and we want your input

The old SkinVaults had too many separate things and too many bugs. The new one will be smaller, faster, easier to use and easy to find on Google.
We would rather do a few things really well than a lot of things badly. **You decide what goes in.**

### What we are thinking about keeping

1. Compare the price of a skin across all marketplaces
2. Inventory value (your whole inventory in euros)
3. Price alerts (a Discord message or e-mail when a skin drops under your price)
4. Giveaways with real skins
5. Trade-up calculator
6. Deals and arbitrage (where to buy cheap, where to sell high)
7. Wishlist
8. Blog and guides

React with the numbers you care about most (you can pick several).

### What we want to know

- What did you use most on the old site, and what did you miss?
- What was broken or annoying?
- What would you be willing to pay for, and how much per month?
- Do you want a message when the new site goes live? (We will build a waiting list with a countdown.)

### How to tell us

Go to **#feedback** and press a button: **Suggest an idea**, **Add a feature**, **Remove a feature** or **Something else**.
A private channel opens that only you and the team can see. You can also just talk in **#chat**.

### What we will not build

No cases and no gambling games for now. The legal side is complicated and we would rather be safe than sorry.

Thank you, this is your site as much as ours.
